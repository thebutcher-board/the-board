# THE BOARD v1.2.0 — Scouting Controls v1

Focused release: Players / Scouting Department only.

- One-tap Target, Watch, Value and Fade signals directly on every player row.
- Quick notes without opening the player profile.
- Visible projection, ADP-ready field, bye-week-ready field, position rank, league status and injury designation.
- Filters for scouting signal and sort controls.
- Apple-style visual hierarchy designed for fast scanning on tablet and phone.
- Preferences persist locally and remain available to the Decision Engine.

No Front Office, Board, League or History redesign is included in this release.
